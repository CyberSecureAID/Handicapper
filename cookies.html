1.What are cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and secure tokens. Together they let the platform work, keep you signed in, remember your preferences and help us understand how the service is used.

2.Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary, required to run the site, authenticate your session and keep it secure. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preferences, remember choices such as your language and notification settings.
  • Analytics / performance, help us see which features are used so we can improve them; used in aggregate.

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information, and we do not run third-party advertising trackers.

3.Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers (for example, our hosting, authentication and database provider and our payment processor) that may set their own strictly-necessary cookies to deliver their part of the service securely.

4.Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings, and clear local storage at any time. Note that disabling strictly-necessary cookies will prevent core features (such as signing in) from working.

5.Changes

We may update this policy as our technology changes. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above. Questions: privacy@sportsexpectations.io.
