How Sports Expectations works

Everything explained in one place. How the platform works from the moment you arrive, what each plan includes and what it costs, how our analysts operate and earn, and how our prestige system keeps them accountable. We are an informational analytics platform, we organize public sports data. We are not a sportsbook and we do not accept, place or facilitate any wager.

1.From the moment you arrive

When you land on Sports Expectations, you see the featured matches of the day. Tap any match and you open its statistics comparison card: records, table position, recent form, team-versus-team metrics and a clear win-probability estimate built from public data. Nothing is invented, every number comes from real, verifiable sources, and probabilities are presented as estimates, never promises of an outcome.

You can browse every sport and league we cover, compare teams and players, and read the analysis. Creating an account is free; some advanced areas and the analyst signals require a paid plan.

3.Our analysts, the program

Alongside our data engine, real people publish their own sports analysis on the platform. An analyst is not an employee on a fixed contract, it is an independent collaborator who chooses to work, and whose earnings depend entirely on the value they create for the people who follow them.

How to become an analyst

Any registered user can request to become an analyst. Our team reviews the request, and if approved, assigns the person to a sport and activates their analyst profile so they can start publishing signals. We keep a limited roster, the number of seats is finite, so a spot only opens when it is genuinely available.

How analysts earn

When a user decides to follow a specific analyst to receive all of their signals, that user pays $2.00 per month. That amount is split as follows:

$1.00goes to the platform, to keep everything running.
$1.00goes to the analyst every month, like a salary for the work they put in.

In other words: the more people value your work and follow you, the more you earn. There is a direct, honest link between effort and reward.

There is no obligation, but activity matters

No analyst is ever forced to publish signals. You come in because you want to work: if you work, people see your signals, follow you, and you earn; if you don’t, you simply don’t earn. It is that clear.

Because seats are limited, we do ask that active analysts stay active. Our policy is fair and human:

  • Missing a single day is never a problem, life happens.
  • If an analyst goes 30 days without publishing any signal, we reach out first to ask what is going on. We will never remove someone just for going through a hard time.
  • From that contact, the analyst has up to 72 hours to respond and let us know their situation.
  • If, after that window, there is no response at all, the analyst account is closed and the seat is freed, so that others who want to work have the opportunity.

4.Prestige points, accountability

To keep analysts honest and to help you choose who to trust, every analyst carries a public Prestige score. It works on a simple, measurable principle: an analyst is judged by whether their calls actually come true.

  • Every signal an analyst publishes is recorded internally the moment it is posted.
  • When the match ends, our system checks the real result through the data feed and evaluates the signal: did the team the analyst backed actually win?
  • Correct call → +1 prestige point. Wrong call, or a draw when a win was predicted, → −1 prestige point.
  • Prestige can go negative. An analyst who keeps missing will visibly fall below zero.

You cannot game it. Because the prediction is recorded when it is published, deleting a signal minutes before a match ends does not erase the result, the outcome still counts. This protects followers from analysts trying to hide their misses.

5.Likes & dislikes

On top of the objective prestige score, the community can react to each signal with a like or a dislike. This is a softer, human signal of acceptance: it reflects how much people appreciate an analyst’s reasoning and style, and helps good analysts stand out. Prestige measures accuracy; likes measure trust and approval.

6.What this all means for you

Put together, the system is designed to be fair on every side: you get transparent, measurable information about who is worth following; analysts get a real, honest way to earn from their work; and the platform stays sustainable. We show the data, we track the results, and we let the numbers speak.
