Responsible Gaming & Eligibility

Last updated: September 2, 2026

We provide statistics, not a place to bet. But because some of our users also wager on unaffiliated platforms, we want to be clear about eligibility and where to get help.

1.Age and jurisdiction

You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your location, which may be 21) to use Sports Expectations. Our service is informational and legal to view in most places, but gambling laws vary widely. It is your responsibility to know and follow the laws that apply to you. We do not target, and our content is not intended for, anyone in a jurisdiction where accessing sports information or wagering is prohibited.

2.We do not encourage wagering

Nothing on this platform is an inducement to gamble. We present information so that people who are already interested in sports can be better informed. We take no position on whether you should wager at all, and we receive nothing from any sportsbook based on your activity.

3.Bet responsibly, if at all

If you choose to gamble on a third-party service: set limits before you start, never chase losses, never wager money you need for essentials, and treat it as entertainment, not income. There is no strategy, and no statistic, that removes risk.

4.Getting help

If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you know, free and confidential help is available. In the United States you can call or text the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-522-4700, available 24/7, or visit ncpgambling.org. Many operators also offer self-exclusion and deposit-limit tools, use them.

5.Self-limiting our service

You may cancel your subscription and stop using Sports Expectations at any time from your account. If you would like your account closed, contact support@sportsexpectations.io.
