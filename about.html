About Sports Expectations

Sports Expectations LLC is a technology company that turns public sports data into clear, verifiable, and useful information. We are an analytics and information platform, not a sportsbook, not a betting operator, and not a financial advisor.

·Who we are

Sports Expectations is a digital platform dedicated to the collection, organization, and presentation of sports statistics and probability estimates. We monitor competitions in real time across multiple sports and leagues, structure the data, run it through consistent analytical methods, and deliver it in a format anyone can understand in seconds rather than hours.

Our work is strictly informational. We do not accept, place, hold, or facilitate wagers of any kind, and nothing on our platform should be interpreted as a recommendation to bet or as a guarantee of any result.

·Our mission

To make sports data honest, accessible, and measurable. In an industry crowded with inflated claims and false certainty, our commitment is the opposite: show the real numbers, explain how they are derived, present probabilities as the estimates they are, and let people make their own informed decisions.

·The people behind it

Sports Expectations was founded by two professionals whose partnership predates the company by more than a decade.

Oscar Luis, based in the United States, leads the company’s vision and operations. He originated the business concept and works hands-on as a baseball data analyst, a discipline he has developed over years of studying the numbers that drive the sport.

Jesús Pérez serves as the founder and developer of the platform. Over the past decade he has built expertise across multiple programming languages, from Python and JavaScript to blockchain, along with graphic design and hands-on experience as a trader. He designed and engineered the entire ecosystem that powers the product today.

What began as a friendship between two colleagues, going back more than a decade, became a company built on a simple division of strengths: one who reads the data, one who builds the systems that make it useful.

·What we build

Our platform combines two things. First, an automated analytics engine that ingests public data, records, standings, form, and match context, and produces clear statistical comparisons and win-probability estimates for upcoming and live games. Second, a network of independent analysts who publish their own sports analysis and signals, and whose track record is measured objectively through a public prestige system.

Together, these give our users a transparent picture of what the data says and of who, among our analysts, has actually been getting it right.

·Our principles

  • Honesty over hype. We present estimates, not promises. We never guarantee an outcome.
  • Transparency. Our probabilities come from real, public data, and our analysts are held to a measurable, public accountability system.
  • We are not a betting service. We do not take wagers or advise anyone to gamble. What people do with information is their own decision and their own responsibility.
  • Responsibility. We encourage responsible, lawful use of our platform and provide clear guidance and resources on the subject.
