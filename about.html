About us

Sports Expectations is an information platform built by two lifelong friends. This is our story, and what we stand for.

Sports Expectations was born from something simple and real: a friendship. Two Cubans who met more than thirteen years ago, back home, and who never let distance break what they built together.

·Two people, one idea

Oscar Luis lives in the United States and leads the vision behind the project. He brought the business idea, backs the operation, and works hands-on as a baseball data analyst, a discipline he has refined over years of studying the numbers behind the game.

Jesús Pérez works from Cuba, where, despite every difficulty his country places in the way, he has spent the last decade becoming a developer across many programming languages, from Python and JavaScript to blockchain, along with graphic design and years of experience as a trader. He designed and built the entire ecosystem you are using right now.

·How it started

Life took them to different countries, but the friendship stayed. One day, talking like they always had, the conversation turned to what each of them loved: Oscar to sports statistics and the craft of reading data, Jesús to building systems that turn raw information into something useful and clear. They realized that between the two of them, they had everything a real product needs.

So they decided to build it. A project where a friendship that survived thousands of miles could also create value, each of them contributing what he does best.

·What we built

The result is a platform that monitors sports statistics in real time, organizes them, runs them through careful analysis, and presents them so that anyone can be better informed in seconds instead of hours. We are not a betting service and we do not tell anyone to wager. We do one thing, and we try to do it exceptionally well: turn public sports data into clear, honest, usable information.

That is who we are, two friends, their knowledge, and a system built to make sense of the numbers.
