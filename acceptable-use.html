Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy sets the rules for using Sports Expectations. It protects our data, our analysts and every honest user. Breaking it can cost you your access without a refund.

Contents

  1. The core rule
  2. Prohibited conduct
  3. Analyst and contributor conduct
  4. Enforcement

1.The core rule

You may use the platform for your own personal, lawful, non-commercial information needs. You may not use it to build a competing product, to harm the service, or to take what we built and give or sell it to others.

2.Prohibited conduct

  • Copying, scraping, harvesting, bulk-downloading, framing or systematically extracting our statistics, signals or content by any automated or manual means.
  • Reselling, redistributing or republishing our information, including reposting picks or statistics in paid or free external groups, channels or sites.
  • Sharing, selling or transferring your account or access credentials.
  • Reverse-engineering, probing or interfering with the platform, its security or its infrastructure.
  • Uploading unlawful, infringing, deceptive, hateful or harmful content, or impersonating others.
  • Using the service where it is prohibited by law.

3.Analyst and contributor conduct

Users who publish signals or commentary must post their own honest analysis, must not fabricate data or track records, and must not use the platform to defraud or mislead followers. See the Terms of Service for the full analyst terms.

4.Enforcement

We actively monitor for misuse, in particular the unauthorized resale of our statistics. If we reasonably believe you have violated this policy we may, at our sole discretion and without prior notice, warn you, restrict or suspend access, revoke your license, terminate your account without refund, and pursue any remedy available at law. We fully disassociate ourselves from anyone who obtains or resells our information in breach of these rules.
