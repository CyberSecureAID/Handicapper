Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Sports Expectations works.

Short version: we sell organized information, not outcomes. We are not a betting or gambling service, we make no guarantees, and subscriptions are non-refundable because the information is delivered the moment you get access. See our Terms and Privacy Policy for the full detail.
What is Sports Expectations?
Sports Expectations is an information platform. We take publicly available sports data and do the heavy lifting for you: we collect it, clean it, organize it and run it through statistical comparisons and probability estimates, so you can be better informed in seconds instead of hours. That is the entire service, organized information.
Is this a betting or gambling site?
No. We do not take bets, hold money for wagering, or run any game of chance. Nothing here is an instruction, recommendation or invitation to gamble or invest. We simply show you organized statistics; what you decide to do with that information is entirely your own choice and your own responsibility.
Do you guarantee that I will win?
No, and we never will. Sports are unpredictable. Our probability estimates are exactly that, estimates built from historical and public data. They are meant to inform your own judgment, not to promise any result. Anyone who guarantees wins is not being honest with you.
Why don’t you offer refunds?
Because you are paying for access to information and to the work of organizing it, not for an outcome. The service is digital and is delivered the instant your subscription is active, so the value has already been provided to you. We are not a betting or investment service, so a result you got on some other platform is not a defect in ours. You can read the full explanation in section 7A of our Terms of Service. This does not waive any right you cannot legally waive, such as the service simply not being delivered.
What do the plans include?
Basic gives you access to all leagues, team and player stat comparisons and honest win probabilities. Pro adds daily picks (hits, goals, points and shots) and a preview of analyst signals. Premium unlocks the full daily board and lets you view every analyst signal. Exact features are always shown on the pricing screen before you pay.
What are analyst signals, and what does “follow” cost?
Analysts publish picks with a short explanation of why they see an edge. Following an analyst costs $2/month and sends their picks to your personal inbox with notifications. Analysts only post when they see a genuine high-probability opportunity, they are not required to post every day.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel at any time from your account. Cancellation stops future renewals and takes effect at the end of your current billing period, you keep access until then.
Where does your data come from and is it accurate?
From publicly available sports sources. We work hard to keep everything accurate and up to date, but public data can contain errors or gaps, and we present the information “as is.” Always use it as one input to your own decision, not as a certainty.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. Payments are handled by a trusted third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card details.

Write to us at support@sportsexpectations.io and we’ll get back to you. For legal matters, use legal@sportsexpectations.io.
